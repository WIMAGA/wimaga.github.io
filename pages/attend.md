## **<span style="color:$gray-800">Registration</span>**
Registration for the workshop can be done through ICASSP 2025 registration portal. More information on about registration fees and how to register for the workshop is available at [https://2025.ieeeicassp.org/registration/](https://2025.ieeeicassp.org/registration/). Please note that the registration timeline and deadlines for ICASSP main conference will also apply to workshop attendance. 

Please note that if you registered for the main ICASSP conference, free attendance at workshops is included. Every paper presented at a workshop must have a non-student registration associated with it. This can be either a main conference author registration or a non-student workshop registration. Main conference author registration will cover up to 4 papers, including main conference and workshop papers. Workshop registration will cover only workshop papers and will NOT cover main conference papers. 

We look forward to your attendance at the workhop in Hyderabad, India on 7th April, 2025.
